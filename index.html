Hello, I'm Ankit.

I'm a graphic designer who likes a clear idea more than a loud one.

I started out making posters for friends' events and never really stopped. Today I work on brand identities, packaging and editorial pieces, mostly in Illustrator, Photoshop and InDesign.

My philosophy: good design should be easy to understand and hard to forget. I start with the problem, sketch on paper, and only then open the software.

My approach: listen, research, sketch, test a few directions, refine one. I share work early so you're never surprised at the end.
